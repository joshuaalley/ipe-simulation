{\large\bfseries MNC Decision Forms} \hfill (Phase 3+)\par 
You own this firm -- alone or with a partner -- even though it sits in another country. Each round: choose how much to produce (\emph{scale}), whether to \emph{relocate} to a new host, and (Phase 4+) whether to pay the fixed cost to \emph{export}. The profit is yours; the output counts for whichever country hosts you. Productivity tiers: HIGH 1.3, MED 1.0, LOW 0.7.
