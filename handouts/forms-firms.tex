{\large\bfseries MNC Decision Form} \hfill (Phase 3+)\par
You own this firm --- alone or with a partner --- even though it sits in another country. Each round: choose how much to produce (\emph{scale}), whether to \emph{relocate} to a new host, and (Phase 4+) whether to pay the fixed cost to \emph{export}. It can move anywhere except your own country. Productivity tiers: HIGH 1.3, MED 1.0, LOW 0.7.
\vspace{6pt}

\noindent\fbox{\begin{minipage}{0.97\linewidth}
\vspace{3pt}
{\large\bfseries ROUND \blank{1.2cm} --- FIRM F1: Cloth-A}
\hfill Owner(s): \blank{4.8cm}\par
\vspace{3pt}
Industry: \textbf{cloth} \quad
Productivity: \textbf{1.3} \quad
Starting host: \textbf{Bosque}\par
Price per unit sold: \textbf{1.00} \quad
Unit cost: \textbf{0.60} \quad
Max scale: \textbf{40} \quad
Export fixed cost: \textbf{8}\par
\vspace{8pt}
SCALE (0--40): \blank{2.2cm} \qquad
RELOCATE TO: \blank{3.4cm} \textit{(blank = stay)}\par
{\small\itshape Relocate anywhere except your own country --- no re-shoring.}\par
\vspace{6pt}
EXPORT this round? \quad \boxx\ Yes \quad \boxx\ No
\hfill\textit{(Phase 4+; pays the fixed cost)}\par
\vspace{3pt}
\end{minipage}}

\vspace{8pt}
{\large\bfseries How the numbers work --- a worked example at scale 23}\par
\textit{Scale 23 is an illustration, not a recommendation. Redo the table at the
scale you actually choose.}

\vspace{6pt}
\begin{center}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.25}
\begin{tabular}{lrr}
\toprule
 & \textbf{Stay home} & \textbf{Export} (premium 25\%) \\
\midrule
Units made: scale $\times$ productivity & 23 $\times$ 1.3 $=$ 29.9 & 29.9 \\
Revenue: units $\times$ price & 29.9 $\times$ 1.00 $=$ 29.90
  & 29.90 $\times$ 1.25 $=$ 37.38 \\
Production cost: scale $\times$ unit cost & 23 $\times$ 0.60 $=$ 13.80 & 13.80 \\
Export fixed cost & --- & 8.00 \\
\midrule
\textbf{Profit} & \textbf{16.10} & \textbf{15.58} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\textbf{Verdict:} at scale 23, exporting \textbf{loses 0.52}: the extra revenue (7.48) does not cover the fixed cost (8.00). Stay home.

\vspace{4pt}
\begin{itemize}[nosep,leftmargin=1.2em]
  \item \textbf{The rule:} export only if units $\times$ price $\times$ premium
    $>$ the fixed export cost. The fixed cost is the same whether you ship 5 units
    or 50, so exporting needs volume.
  \item \textbf{The premium} is on the board each round. 25\% is the most it
    can be; tariffs other countries put on your good from your host shrink it.
  \item \textbf{Your local sales} --- 20\% of your units --- count toward your
    \emph{host's} welfare, export or not; the rest sells on world markets.
    \textbf{Your profit} counts every unit, and it is yours alone.
  \item \textbf{Your host's MNC tax} takes its \% of your revenue (premium
    included), so a lower-tax host keeps more of your profit --- but moving
    means producing nothing that round. From Phase 5 your profit is worth what
    your host's currency is worth.
\end{itemize}

It can move anywhere except your own country. Productivity tiers: HIGH 1.3, MED 1.0, LOW 0.7.

\textbf{Verdict:} at scale 23, exporting \textbf{loses 3.97}: the extra revenue (4.03) does not cover the fixed cost (8.00). Stay home.

\vspace{4pt}
\begin{itemize}[nosep,leftmargin=1.2em]
  \item \textbf{The rule:} export only if units $\times$ price $\times$ premium
    $>$ the fixed export cost. The fixed cost is the same whether you ship 5 units
    or 50, so exporting needs volume.
  \item \textbf{The premium} is on the board each round. 25\% is the most it
    can be; tariffs other countries put on your good from your host shrink it.
  \item \textbf{Your local sales} --- 20\% of your units --- count toward your
    \emph{host's} welfare, export or not; the rest sells on world markets.
    \textbf{Your profit} counts every unit, and it is yours alone.
  \item \textbf{Your host's MNC tax} takes its \% of your revenue (premium
    included), so a lower-tax host keeps more of your profit --- but moving
    means producing nothing that round. From Phase 5 your profit is worth what
    your host's currency is worth.
\end{itemize}
